ABOUT WM_CHECKLIST

WM_CHECKLIST started from a simple personal need. I use Capacities for general notes, but for everyday purchase tracking, planning, installments, and credit limits I wanted a dedicated, fast, distraction-free tool.

Most existing apps either lock features behind subscriptions or clutter the screen with ads. So I built this tracker for myself first — zero servers, zero tracking, zero ads — and open-sourced it for free on GitHub. Fork it, customize it, or share your ideas anytime.
